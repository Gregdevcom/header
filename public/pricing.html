Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our plans

How does the free trial work?

When you sign up for the Everyday plan, you get full access to all features for 7 days completely free. You will be asked for your payment details before starting. If you choose to cancel before the 7 day period, you will get moved down to our Basic plan - hustle free.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Absolutely! You can upgrade from Basic to Everyday at any time and get immediate access to all premium features. If you decide to downgrade, your premium features will remain active until the end of your current billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and Apple Pay. All payments are processed securely through Stripe. We never store your full credit card details on our servers.

What happens if I use all my daily summaries?

If you reach your daily limit, you'll see a friendly notification letting you know. Your limit resets at midnight (UTC) every day. On the Basic plan, you can always upgrade to Everyday to get 50 summaries per day. Pro tip: Remember, you can always store your favorite articles by pressing the star button - they are accessible anytime, no matter your limit.

Is there a refund policy?

You can try Header free for 7 days. Your card will not be charged during the trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, you won't be charged. Unfortunately, all payments after the trial are non-refundable.

Do unused summaries roll over?

Currently, daily summaries don't roll over to the next day. Each day starts fresh with your full allocation. This keeps things simple and ensures you're always getting the most current news. We find most users prefer fresh daily content anyway!
